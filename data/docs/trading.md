# Trading Strategies

## NIFTY 50 Options

### Long Straddle
- Entry: At-the-money (ATM) strikes
- Buy both Call and Put simultaneously
- Profit from large price movements in either direction
- Best used before major events (earnings, RBI policy, budget)

### Short Straddle
- Sell ATM Call and Put
- Collect premium as income
- Risk: Unlimited if price moves sharply
- Best in low-volatility, range-bound markets

### Long Strangle
- Buy OTM Call and OTM Put
- Cheaper than straddle, needs bigger move to profit
- Suitable for moderate volatility expectations

## Bitcoin Long Straddle Setup
- Exchange: Any major crypto exchange (Deribit recommended for options)
- Select ATM strike closest to current BTC price
- Buy 1 Call + 1 Put with same expiry (weekly preferred)
- Target: 5%+ move in either direction
- Stop loss: If premium decays 50%, exit both legs

## Risk Management Guidelines
- Never risk more than 2% of capital per trade
- Set stop loss at 1.5x entry premium for straddles
- Use trailing stop once position is 50% in profit
- Max 3 open positions at any time
- Avoid trading during illiquid hours (late night for NIFTY)

## Entry/Exit Timing
- NIFTY: Enter 9:20-9:45 AM IST after opening volatility settles
- Exit by 3:15 PM IST to avoid closing auction risk
- Bitcoin: 24/7 market, avoid low-liquidity weekends
- Best entry: Post major news events when IV is elevated
- Best exit: When 60-70% of max profit is achieved

## Greeks to Monitor
- Delta: Keep near-zero for straddles (delta-neutral)
- Theta: Time decay works against buyers, monitor daily
- Vega: High vega = sensitive to IV changes; favors buyers pre-event
- Gamma: Increases near expiry; both risk and opportunity
